Research Interests

My interests focus on object detection / semantic segmentation under challenging conditions (e.g. night, rain, snow, and fog). Currently, I aim to explore parameter-efficient fine-tuning of multimodal foundation models for downstream tasks, with a particular focus on domain adaptation/generalization and test-time adaptation under distribution shifts.
